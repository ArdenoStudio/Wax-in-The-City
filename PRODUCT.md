# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Women in Colombo looking for a private, hygienic, premium waxing, facial, and skin-care salon experience. They may be first-time waxers, regular maintenance clients, or guests who value privacy over a busy open salon floor. They are likely comparing trust signals quickly: location, hygiene, service fit, WhatsApp availability, price starting points, and whether the studio feels safe enough to book.

## Product Purpose

This website exists to make Wax In The City SL feel sharper, more glamorous, and more trustworthy than a generic salon listing. Success means a visitor can understand the studio promise within the first viewport, confirm the branch and service fit without friction, and move confidently to WhatsApp or a booking request without feeling oversold.

## Brand Personality

Sharper, glamorous, private, careful. The brand should feel like a polished appointment studio rather than a mass-market beauty parlor: composed, skin-aware, women-first, and quietly confident, with enough drama to feel memorable.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic pink salon templates, overdone glass and cursor effects, fake luxury wording, stock-photo sameness, unverified review claims, and UI that feels like a copied AI landing page. Avoid noisy decoration that competes with the client decision: privacy, hygiene, location, service, and booking.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with privacy and confidence. The first screen must communicate that this is a ladies-only, appointment-led studio where guests are handled carefully.
2. Glamour should be precise, not loud. Use rich contrast, disciplined typography, confident imagery, and controlled motion instead of glittery decoration.
3. Trust beats theatrics. Hygiene, branch details, WhatsApp paths, and honest form behavior have priority over visual tricks.
4. Make booking feel low-risk. Every booking path should clarify that requests are reviewed first and urgent needs should go through WhatsApp.
5. Do not fake proof. Reviews, addresses, photos, and claims must be either verified or visibly treated as content pending client confirmation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast, focus visibility, keyboard navigation, and form labeling. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Avoid interface patterns that hide the native pointer, trap focus, rely on color alone, or make mobile visitors work through decorative motion before they can book.
